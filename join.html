Find your professional home.

Membership in AAPHD is open to all individuals concerned with improving the oral health of the public. There are several membership categories based upon affiliation in dental public health.

All membership categories are on a calendar year basis, January 1 – December 31. While AAPHD does not prorate membership dues, anyone who joins / renews after October 1 will receive the remainder of the calendar year as well as the next calendar year for the same membership fee.

Individual membership

Choose the category that fits.

All categories include the Journal of Public Health Dentistry, member-only website sections, and conference registration discounts at NOHC.

Resident or Student

$55/ year

Dental Public Health Residents, Other Dental Residents, and Students in dental, hygiene, therapy, or public health programs. Must be enrolled at least half-time in an accredited educational program.

  • Committee service eligibility
  • Journal publication access
  • Member website sections
  • Student / resident conference rates
Join as student

Associate

$200/ year

Any non-dentist individual with an interest in dental public health — dental assistants, hygienists, therapists, physicians, nurses, researchers, administrators, and more.

  • Committee participation & associate liaison board service
  • Journal access & member website sections
  • Conference registration discounts
Join as associate

Dentist (Full)

$275/ year

DDS, DMD, or equivalent degree, plus one of: (1) completion of a CODA-accredited advanced education program in dental public health; (2) advanced public health training (e.g., MPH); or (3) sufficiently demonstrated experience or interest in dental public health.

  • Voting privileges in board elections & bylaws
  • Committee & board service eligibility
  • Journal of Public Health Dentistry (print & digital)
  • Member-only website sections
  • NOHC member registration rate
Join as full member

Life Member

$0(lifetime)

An AAPHD Dentist or Associate member in good standing who has achieved 20 years of continuous membership and is no longer practicing public health or preventive dentistry. Requires submission of a Lifetime Membership Application for office approval.

  • Full committee participation & leadership roles
  • Journal access & member website sections
  • Conference discounts
Apply for life membership

Higher contribution levels available. Dentist and Associate members may select Contributing, Sustaining, or Sponsoring — same eligibility, with additional support for AAPHD's organizational, advocacy, and infrastructure work.
