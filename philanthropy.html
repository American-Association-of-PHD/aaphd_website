AAPHD Philanthropic Efforts

Advancing oral health through education, research and service.

Donate

The AAPHD, through scholarships, research grants and support for student chapters, invests in leaders working to improve the oral health of the public.

The AAPHD maintains several named funds honoring individual oral health leaders. These include the Herschel S. Horowitz Scholarships for dentists and dental hygienists, the Dr. R. Gary Rozier Memorial Fund, the Brian Burt Memorial Fund, the Helen Doherty Student Chapter Fund and the ABDPH Lotzkar Fund. Together they support future specialists and public health dentistry professionals, fund new research and help keep the field strong for the communities it serves. Gifts are tax-deductible, and AAPHD welcomes planned gifts and donor-advised fund grants.

Scholarships

Help train future dental public health leaders. Horowitz and Rozier scholarships help dentists and dental hygienists pay for graduate study in public health.

See the scholarships
  • Herschel S. Horowitz ScholarshipAnnual scholarships for dentists and dental hygienists pursuing graduate study in public health.
  • Dr. R. Gary Rozier Memorial FundScholarships, research and training for residents and early-career professionals in dental public health.

Small Grants

Fund new ideas. Grants of up to $10,000 support new education and research projects that improve oral health outcomes and often lead to larger funded work.

Named Funds

Honor the leaders who built the field. Gifts to the Horowitz, Rozier, Burt, Doherty and Lotzkar funds carry their legacy forward through scholarships, student chapters and specialty board certification.

See the named funds
  • Herschel S. Horowitz ScholarshipHonoring Dr. Herschel S. Horowitz, a dental epidemiologist, educator and public health advocate, through scholarships for dentists and dental hygienists.
  • Dr. R. Gary Rozier Memorial FundHonoring a former AAPHD president and one of the field's most influential mentors and scholars.
  • Brian Burt Memorial FundHonoring a leader whose work in epidemiology and equity shaped generations of professionals.
  • Helen K. Doherty Student Chapter FundHonoring Helen K. Doherty by supporting AAPHD student chapters and the next generation of professionals.
  • ABDPH Lotzkar FundHonoring Dr. Stanley Lotzkar’s 21 years of service to the American Board of Dental Public Health.
