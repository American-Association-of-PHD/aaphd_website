New special issue

Forward Together: Advancing Health Equity in Uncertain Times

Guest editors: Josefine Ortiz Wolfe, PhD, RDH · Eleanor Fleming, PhD, DDS, MPH

This supplement gathers research, reviews, and commentaries that keep health equity central to dental public health at a challenging moment for the field — spanning community water fluoridation and misinformation, safety-net innovation, curricular reform, workforce expansion through dental therapists and health aides, disability equity, philanthropy, and organizational practice. It closes with a call to stay the course.

Funded by CareQuest Institute for Oral Health.

About the journal

The Journal of Public Health Dentistry is devoted to the advancement of public health dentistry through the exploration of related research, practice, and policy developments. We publish original research, methods articles, and review articles that support the growth and practical application of the dental public health knowledge base. We are an official publication of the American Association of Public Health Dentistry.

AAPHD members receive complimentary print and digital access as part of their membership.
