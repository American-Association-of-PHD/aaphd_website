Institutional Membership

Strengthen your organization's commitment to dental public health.

Affiliating with AAPHD demonstrates commitment to oral health disparities and public health dentistry education. Tiered packages extend benefits to faculty and students within budget.

View membership tiers See current members
Membership Tiers

Choose the right tier for your organization.

All tiers include logo placement, discounted conference registration, and access to members-only sections of the website. Faculty development access to national experts, webinars, policy updates, and collaborative opportunities.

  • 1 free job post
  • Logo placement
