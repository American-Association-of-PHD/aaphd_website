Welcome to AAPHD

Improving total health through oral health.

Because oral health is essential to overall health — and every person, in every community, deserves to achieve it. AAPHD unites and empowers the professionals making that future real.

DPH Reference Materials Committee

AAPHD is pleased to announce the launch of its Dental Public Health Reference Materials Committee, a newly created standing committee. The committee's first reference lists — organized by DPH competency — are expected at NOHC 2027 and will be updated annually thereafter.
