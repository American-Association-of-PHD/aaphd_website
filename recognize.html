Merit Awards

Premier recognition for the field

Multiple categories spanning research, practice, leadership, and lifetime contribution.

Distinguished Service Award

Sustained, exemplary service to AAPHD and the field of dental public health.

Award of Merit

Outstanding contribution to dental public health practice, research, or policy.

John W. Knutson Distinguished Service Award

The Association's highest honor for a lifetime of service to the field.

Student Awards

Recognizing the next generation

AAPHD's student award programs support and celebrate emerging dental public health scholars.

Student Merit Award

Recognizes a graduating student who has demonstrated leadership, scholarship, and commitment to dental public health.

Predoctoral Dental Student Award

For predoctoral students with outstanding work in dental public health.

Leverett Graduate Student Award

For graduate students advancing dental public health research and practice.
