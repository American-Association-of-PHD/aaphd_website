AAPHD Educational Webinar Series

The AAPHD Educational Webinar Series is designed to share knowledge, spark discussion, and highlight expertise from across the dental public health community. Most webinars are free and open to both AAPHD members and non-members. CE credit is available to those who attend the live webinar and complete the session survey.

Dental Care in Canada: A System in Transition

Dr. Anil Menon, President, Canadian Association of Public Health Dentistry (CAPHD)

Wednesday, November 4, 2026 · 11 AM CST / 12 PM EST

Register now

Description

Canada is often described internationally as having a publicly funded health care system, yet dental care has historically developed largely outside Medicare. Most oral health services have been financed through private insurance and out-of-pocket payment, alongside a patchwork of federal and provincial public programs targeted to specific populations.

That landscape is now changing. The introduction of the Canadian Dental Care Plan represents the largest expansion of publicly financed dental coverage in Canada in decades and raises important questions about access, workforce capacity, provider participation, federal-provincial responsibilities, and the future balance between public and private dental care.

This presentation will provide an overview of how dental care in Canada is financed, governed, and delivered, explain the roles of federal and provincial governments, examine recent reforms including the Canadian Dental Care Plan, and discuss persistent challenges in access and oral health equity. Particular attention will be given to similarities and differences between the Canadian and United States systems and to lessons emerging from Canada’s current policy transition.

Learning objectives

By the end of the session, participants will be able to:

  • Describe how dental care is financed, governed, and delivered within the Canadian health system.
  • Explain the roles of federal, provincial/territorial, private insurance, and out-of-pocket financing in Canadian dental care.
  • Summarize the significance of the Canadian Dental Care Plan and its potential implications for access and the dental delivery system.
  • Compare key features of the Canadian and United States approaches to dental care and identify areas of shared policy concern.

Who should attend

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental and dental hygiene students, residents, educators, researchers, public health professionals, policymakers, and others interested in dental public health and population oral health in Canada.

About the speaker

Dr. Anil Menon, BDS, MBA, MSc, FRSPH, FRCD(C)

Dr. Anil Menon is a Dental Public Health Specialist and Assistant Professor at the University of Manitoba. He is the current President of the Canadian Association of Public Health Dentistry (CAPHD), where he provides national leadership on oral health equity, prevention, access to care, and public health policy. His work spans research, teaching, and advocacy aimed at improving oral health outcomes for underserved populations in Canada.
