2027 award nominations are now open

AAPHD Awards of Merit — call for nominations

Nominations are due January 15, 2027.

There is no greater acknowledgement than that which comes from our peers. Please help the AAPHD Awards Committee select the recipients for its 2027 Awards of Merit. We are seeking nominations for two awards:

Distinguished Service Award

Presented to a member of AAPHD for excellent and distinguished service to public health dentistry and the association.

Public Service Award

Presented to an individual for substantial contribution through action related to public health dentistry issues.

Send your letter of nomination along with a short paragraph explaining why you believe this person deserves this award. Please include the nominee’s information.

Nominations should be sent by January 15, 2027 to AAPHD at info.NO-SPAM@aaphd.removeme.org.
