Student Merit Awards

Nominations for the 2027 Student Merit Awards are closed.

Provide national recognition and cash prizes to predoctoral dental students, dental hygiene students, and graduate students / residents in dental public health for outstanding projects that demonstrate impact in community oral health.

Awardees will present their projects during the General Poster Session at the 2027 National Oral Health Conference® (NOHC) and be recognized at the AAPHD Annual Business Meeting on Sunday, April 11, 2027.

Each award category includes

Eligibility
Senior or recently graduated students who completed a community-based or research project before graduation.
Submission requirements
Online submission of an abstract, 20-page report, letter of nomination, IRB documentation (if applicable), and a cover page with contact information.
Evaluation criteria
Originality, design, data quality, community impact, student leadership, and relevance to dental public health.
Awards
First ($500), Second ($200), and Third Place ($100) plus complimentary 2027 NOHC registration, AAPHD membership for 2027, electronic access for one year to the Journal of Public Health Dentistry (JPHD) and a print copy subscription of the JPHD for one year.

Student Recognition Awards in Community Dentistry

2027 Community Award Nominations will open in January, 2027

Honors senior dental and dental hygiene students demonstrating commitment to community service through impactful projects in public health or community-based settings.

Recognized work can span a wide range of projects, service roles, advocacy efforts, or sustained community engagement. Projects may include (but are not limited to):

  • Community-based research or needs assessments
  • Service-learning or outreach initiatives
  • Public health education or advocacy
  • Quality improvement or access-to-care projects
  • Longitudinal volunteer or leadership efforts
Eligibility
One senior student per accredited dental school and per dental hygiene program (baccalaureate-level only) in the U.S. and Canada.
Nomination process
Each school may nominate one student who has completed a meaningful community-focused project or service initiative. Students are encouraged to share this opportunity with faculty or program directors that are familiar with their work.
Awards
Recipients will receive an electronic award certificate in a printable PDF format that will be presented at the school’s commencement or award ceremony, complimentary 2027 Membership, and a one-year electronic subscription to the Journal of Public Health Dentistry.
