Trainer & Speaker Network

Speak. Train. Lead.

A network of AAPHD members available to speak, lead workshops, and train teams on dental public health topics. Available to invited audiences nationwide.

For organizers

Looking for a speaker?

Tell us about your event — audience, format, topic interest, dates — and AAPHD will match you with members from our network.

For members

Want to be in the network?

AAPHD members in good standing are welcome to join the Trainer and Speaker Network. Submit your areas of expertise, formats you're available for, and travel preferences.
